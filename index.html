Meet the Founder

Matimba Mthombeni — Founder & CEO

Matimba Mthombeni is the visionary behind Dr Scenteology Fragrances. Driven by a passion for distinctive scents and a belief that fragrance is a form of self-expression, he founded the brand to make premium-quality perfumes accessible to everyone in South Africa.

Under his leadership, Dr Scenteology has grown into a trusted name, offering carefully curated fragrances that reflect confidence, elegance, and individuality. His commitment to quality and customer satisfaction remains at the heart of everything the company does.
